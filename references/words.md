# Words

Word lists for pattern #12, merged from humanizer 3.1.0, stop-slop and no-ai-slop. Words change with every model release, so structures (references/patterns.md) matter more than any list. Leave a listed word alone inside a quotation, a name or a title, and when it carries its technical meaning (see Keep, at the end).

## 1. Cut on sight

Tier 1 in every mode. Models use these far more often than people do, and the plain version is always better.

delve, tapestry, testament, realm, beacon, multifaceted, paramount, meticulous, meticulously, intricate, intricacies, interplay, underscore (verb), showcase (verb), garner, bolster, bolstered, pivotal, vibrant, enduring, embark, elevate, supercharge, harness (figurative), ever-evolving, transformative, paradigm shift, game changer, game-changer, cutting-edge, foster, leverage (verb), utilize, facilitate, empower, streamline, "this is huge", "this changes everything"

## 2. Cut when decorative

Tier 2. Each has a plain use. Cut it when it is figurative or decorative, or when two or more words from these lists share a paragraph. Strict mode cuts these on sight too.

actually, additionally, align with, crucial, enhance, key (adjective), landscape (abstract), highlight (verb), quietly, robust, valuable, gate or gated (figurative), deep dive

## 3. Jargon to plain words

Replace with plain language. If the plain word feels empty, the sentence had nothing to say.

| Avoid | Use |
|-------|-----|
| navigate (challenges) | handle, deal with |
| unpack | explain, look at |
| lean into | accept, commit to |
| landscape (as context) | situation, market, field |
| game changer | say what changed, and by how much |
| double down | commit, do more of |
| deep dive | close look, analysis |
| take a step back | reconsider |
| moving forward, going forward | next, from now on |
| circle back | return to, revisit |
| on the same page | agreed |
| in order to | to |
| with regard to | about |

## 4. Often-empty adverbs

Calibrated mode: cut them when they add nothing; keep them when they carry real emphasis, doubt, contrast or SV's spoken rhythm. Strict mode: cut every -ly adverb and every word here unless deleting it changes the facts.

really, just, literally, genuinely, honestly, simply, actually, deeply, truly, fundamentally, inherently, inevitably, interestingly, importantly, crucially, basically, totally, absolutely, incredibly

Hinglish intensifiers live in references/hinglish.md.

## 5. Often-empty phrases

Cut them when they delay the point. An occasional one may stay when it is part of SV's recognizable voice and the sentence still earns its place.

at its core, in today's world, in today's fast-paced world, in the age of, in the world of, in a world where, it's worth noting, it's important to note, at the end of the day, when it comes to, the reality is, the truth is, in terms of, in this article, in this post, let's dive in, it turns out

## 6. Performative emphasis

False intimacy or manufactured sincerity. Cut.

"I promise", "trust me on this", "creeps in", "and that's okay"

## Keep

These words keep their technical or platform meaning and are never tells in that sense:

1. landscape: video orientation (16:9 landscape vs 9:16 vertical).
2. key: API key, keyframe, YouTube's key moments.
3. highlight: a highlights reel or clip.
4. engagement: the metric.
5. gated: gated content or a gated lead magnet.
6. robust: a statistical term.
7. hook: the opening seconds of a video or first line of a post; a craft term.
8. journey: SV uses it naturally (see voice/profile.md).
